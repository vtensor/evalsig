# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want EVALSIG to be a project where everyone, regardless of
experience, background, or affiliation, feels welcome to contribute. To
that end, we hold ourselves and every participant to a high standard of
courtesy and professionalism.

## Standards

Expected:

* Use welcoming, inclusive language.
* Disagree with the idea, not the person.
* Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
* Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
* Show empathy toward other contributors, especially those new to the
  community.

Not acceptable:

* Personal attacks, insults, or other harassment.
* Trolling, deliberately inflammatory comments, or sustained disruption
  of discussion.
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
* Sexualised language or imagery; unwelcome sexual attention or
  advances.
* Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces, including GitHub issues and
pull requests, the documentation site comments (when enabled), the
maintainers' email inbox, and any in-person or virtual events run
under the EVALSIG name. It also applies when an individual is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to `conduct@evalsig.dev`. All reports
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and confidentially.

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including:

* A private written warning.
* A public correction or clarification.
* A temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces.
* Removal of contributions.

Decisions can be appealed by emailing `conduct@evalsig.dev` within 14
days. A different maintainer than the original handler will review the
appeal.
